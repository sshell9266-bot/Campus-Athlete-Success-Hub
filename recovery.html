Recovery & Resources

Explore recovery tips and academic resources to help balance athletics and school.

Recovery & Wellness Tips

Rest & Recovery

Give your body time to recover between demanding training sessions.

Sleep

Try to maintain a consistent sleep schedule to support recovery.

Mobility

Include gentle mobility exercises when appropriate.

Hydration

Drink water regularly throughout the day, especially around physical activity.

Academic Success Tips

Time Management

Use a weekly schedule to organize classes, assignments, and practices.

Study Strategies

Break large assignments into smaller tasks and review material regularly.

Academic Support

Contact instructors or campus academic support services when you need help.

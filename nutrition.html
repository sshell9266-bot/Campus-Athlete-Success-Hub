Nutrition & Hydration

Explore basic nutrition and hydration information to support an active lifestyle.

Nutrition Basics

Eating balanced meals can help student-athletes maintain energy throughout the day.

  • Carbohydrates provide energy for workouts and daily activities.
  • Protein supports muscle recovery and maintenance.
  • Fruits and vegetables provide important vitamins and minerals.
  • Healthy fats are part of a balanced diet.

Hydration Basics

Staying hydrated is important for physical activity and everyday health.

  • Drink water regularly throughout the day.
  • Bring a water bottle to classes and workouts.
  • Pay attention to thirst and activity level.
  • Fluid needs can increase during exercise and hot weather.
